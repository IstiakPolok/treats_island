# 🏝️ Treats Island - Client User Guide

Welcome to **Treats Island**! Treats Island is a mobile fundraising platform that makes raising funds sweet, simple, and engaging. By setting up virtual candy "Pop-Up Stores," participants can share their unique store links with supporters to raise money for their schools, sports teams, or organizations.

This guide will walk you through how to use the app, from launching a campaign to setting up your store, sharing links, and tracking your success.

## 🌟 Key Features Overview

*   **90-Second Event Launch**: Setup a group fundraiser with a team name, custom duration, and invite codes.
*   **Virtual Pop-Up Stores**: Personalize your candy storefront with a custom name, goal, store note, and a welcome photo/video.
*   **Easy Social Sharing**: Share your unique store link directly via SMS, email, or social media.
*   **Live Leaderboard**: Friendly competition with real-time tracking of participants and the top fundraisers.
*   **Supporter Tracker**: Instantly see who has supported your store and thank them for their contributions.

## 🚀 Getting Started (Onboarding & Login)

1. **Launch the App**: Open the Treats Island app. You'll see the splash page followed by onboarding cards explaining how the platform works.
2. **Sign Up / Log In**: 
   * Enter your mobile number to receive a **One-Time Password (OTP)**.
   * Input the 6-digit verification code sent to your phone.
   * If you're new, complete your profile by setting your full name and email address.

## 📣 For Organizers: Launching a Fundraiser Campaign

If you are the team leader, coordinator, or coach, you can launch a group fundraiser in under 90 seconds:

1. **Start the Campaign**: On the home screen, tap the **"START A FUNDRAISER"** card.
2. **Name Your Team**: Enter your team or organization name (e.g., *"Westside Tigers U12"*).
3. **Set the Schedule & Duration**: 
   * Select the start date for your campaign.
   * Choose the duration (e.g., *5 days, 7 days*).
4. **Define Team Size**: Enter the estimated number of participants/players who will join the fundraiser.
5. **Launch**: Review the details and schedule/launch the event. A unique **6-Digit Event Code** will be generated.
6. **Invite Your Team**: Share this code with all team members, players, or students so they can join.

## 🎨 For Participants: Joining & Creating Your Pop-Up Store

If you are a player, student, or team member joining a fundraiser:

1. **Enter Event Code**: On the home screen, tap **"ENTER EVENT CODE"** and enter the 6-digit code provided by your organizer.
2. **Set Your Display Name**: Choose the name supporters will see when they visit your shop (e.g., *"Toby's Candy Shop"*).
3. **Set Your Fundraising Goal**: Enter your personal target (e.g., *\$1,200*).
4. **Write a Store Note**: Write a short greeting or explanation of what you are raising money for (e.g., *"Help me raise money for our new soccer jerseys!"*).
5. **Add Visuals (Recommended)**: 
   * Record or upload a short **welcome video** or photo. Supporters love seeing a personal face and message!
6. **Create**: Save your details. Your customized virtual Pop-Up Store is now live!

## 🔗 Sharing Your Store & Raising Funds

Once your store is ready, it's time to get the word out:

1. **Access Your Store**: Navigate to the **Event Overview** screen (accessible from the Home screen's active card).
2. **Get Your Share Link**: Tap the **"Share Link"** button on your store card.
3. **Share Options**:
   * **Copy Link**: Copies your unique URL directly to your clipboard to paste anywhere.
   * **Share Sheet**: Tap the share icon to send the link directly to contacts via text messages (SMS), WhatsApp, Email, Facebook, Instagram, or other apps.
4. **Supporter Experience**: When supporters click your link, they are taken to your personalized storefront where they can purchase delicious treats. Every purchase contributes directly to your goal!

## 📊 Monitoring Campaign Progress & Leaderboards

Keep the momentum going by checking your stats regularly:

*   **Fundraising Progress Bar**: The top of your dashboard displays your total funds raised versus your personal goal in real-time, along with a countdown timer showing the remaining days/hours of the event.
*   **Leaderboard**: Tap the **"Leaderboard"** tab in the event overview to see how you rank against other team members. Healthy competition helps boost overall campaign success!
*   **My Supporters**: Tap the **"My Supporters"** section to view a list of everyone who has bought treats or donated through your store. Use this to send thank-you notes to your supporters.

---

## ⚙️ Managing Your Profile & Settings

To manage your account or edit your store:

*   **Edit Your Store**: Navigate to **"Pop-up store settings"** on the event overview screen to update your store note, change your goal, or upload a new photo/video.
*   **Account Settings**: Tap the **Profile** icon in the bottom navigation bar to update your name, email, notification preferences, or view past events you've participated in.
*   **Terms and Support**: Access the Terms & Conditions and Support information from the Profile menu.
